**Full description:**

```
HUX turns the data already on your phone or watch into simple, daily answers:
how rested you are, how hard to go today, and what to do next. It's built for
Indian life: chai, late dinners, shaadi season, exam weeks, night shifts and
fasting days.

YOUR DAY, IN ONE LOOK
• HUX Charge – your energy for today, from your own sleep and heart data
• Today's effort – push, steady, easy or rest
• A step goal set from YOUR usual, not a one-size 10,000
• Target bedtime to recover well tonight

SLEEP & TRENDS
• Sleep vs your own normal, with 7- and 30-day trends
• Weekly body story: this week vs last, and one thing to try

AI COACH (optional)
• Ask anything about your sleep, energy, steps or food
• Replies in English, Hinglish, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or Kannada
• Choose a style: Coach, Dost, Amma or Just the numbers

FOOD THE INDIAN WAY
• Log meals in katoris and rotis, or snap a photo (optional)
• Protein gap: "add 1 katori dal + paneer" – veg, egg and Jain friendly

MOVE MORE, SIMPLY
• Guided plans with voice cues: First Steps, Brisk 100, Japanese interval
  walking, Zero to 5K, Steady & Strong 60+, Stair snacks
• Walk-after-meal and sit-too-long reminders
• Air-quality and heat alerts for your city, with the best time to walk

HABITS THAT STICK
• Daily journal: see what chai, sweets or late dinners do to YOUR sleep
• Forgiving streaks with rest passes, and a monthly report

LIFE MODES
• Shaadi / big day, exam season, night shift and fasting companion

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your health readings stay on your phone (the coach sees a short
  summary only if you turn it on)
• No ads. Your data is never sold
• Download or delete your data any time, inside the app

Works with Health Connect (Android).

HUX gives wellness insights from your own data. It is not a medical device and
doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. For health concerns, please see a
doctor. In an emergency call 112.
```
