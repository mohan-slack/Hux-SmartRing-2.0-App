Delete your HUX account

HUX — by Viveon Gizit Private Limited

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open HUX and sign in.
  2. Tap the shield icon (Privacy & account) at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

No longer have the app?

Email privacy@hux.co.in from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my HUX account. We will confirm it's you and delete the account within 7 days, then email you to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your HUX account: email address, sign-in details, and linked Google/Apple sign-in.
  • Coach message counts linked to your account.
  • When you delete in the app: everything HUX keeps on that phone — readings, sleep, journal, meals, plans, coach chat and answers.

What is not affected

  • Data in Apple Health or Health Connect. HUX only reads it. Manage it in those apps.
  • HUX data on a phone you no longer have. Uninstalling HUX removes it.
  • Copies held briefly in our hosting provider's backups expire within [X] days.

HUX does not store your health readings, coach chats or meal photos on its servers. See our privacy policy.
